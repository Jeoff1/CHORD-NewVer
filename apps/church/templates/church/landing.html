How it works

Four steps from a theme to a confirmed {{ labels.lineup|lower }}.

  1. 1

    Post the theme

    The {{ labels.pastor|lower }} writes the {{ labels.worship_service|lower }}'s theme and Bible passage, with an optional {{ labels.sermon|lower }} series.

  2. 2

    Get suggestions

    CHORD compares the theme with each song's lyrics and your church's own tags, and ranks the songs that fit.

  3. 3

    Review and confirm

    The worship leader shapes the {{ labels.lineup|lower }}: lock, swap, set keys. The {{ labels.pastor|lower }} has the final word and confirms it.

  4. 4

    Rehearse together

    The team sees the confirmed {{ labels.lineup|lower }} with chord sheets in each person's key, and a Spotify playlist to practise with.

Rehearsal

The sheet, the key and the song, on one page.

Each song in a confirmed {{ labels.lineup|lower }} has its own page: the chord sheet in the key for everyone, your own key and capo, the leader's notes, and the track ready to play.
